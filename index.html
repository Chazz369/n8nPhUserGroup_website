What we do

We build community through technical collaboration, peer problem solving, and reusable automation patterns.

MEETUPS MONTHLY

Local and virtual meetups

We gather regularly in person and online to demonstrate real production workflows and discuss automation patterns. Builders walk through how they connect APIs, manage webhooks, and handle error routing in production.

WORKSHOPS HANDS-ON

Hands-on technical workshops

Step-by-step sessions cover intermediate and advanced topics, from self-hosting n8n on Docker to custom node development and AI agent orchestration. Participants work through live code and shared canvas templates.

WORKFLOWS OPEN SOURCE

Shared workflow library

Members share tested workflow templates tailored to local services, regional APIs, and common operational tasks. Every workflow is open source, documented, and free to adapt for your own instances.
